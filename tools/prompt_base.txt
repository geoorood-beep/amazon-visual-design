[STYLE]
Photorealistic editorial lifestyle photograph for an Amazon listing. Real home, lived-in but tidy. Natural colours, neutral whites, no orange cast.

[LIGHT]
One directional daylight source from a window. Crisp, directional cast shadows on the surface under the product, the pet and the hands. A clear highlight band on the brushed stainless steel with a natural falloff on the far side. No flat shadowless light, no haze, no HDR glow.

[PRODUCT_RULES]
The product must match the attached product reference image exactly: same outline, proportions, parts and part positions. Do not add, remove or restyle any part. Show the whole product in frame unless the card explicitly asks for a close shot or an extreme close-up. Being close to the floor or the counter is not a close shot. Only a close shot or an extreme close-up may crop the product, and every part that remains visible must match the reference, including its shape. Do not use cropping to change the outline. The focused part of the product is sharp, and the product never glows.

[GEOMETRY_RULES]
Follow the size, outline and parts list above. Copy the outline of the product in the attached product reference: do not straighten, bend, stretch, thin, thicken, enlarge or shrink it, and show nothing from the product set that is not in the parts list.

[BASELINE]
Product shape baseline render, not an advertisement. The product alone on a plain seamless mid-grey background (RGB 200,200,200). Soft, even studio light from above, a soft contact shadow, no props, no hands, no pets, no text, no logo. Long lens, no wide-angle distortion.

[BASELINE_RULES]
Copy the shape, proportions, thickness, edges and surface texture from the attached real photos exactly. Where the photos do not show a side or a part, keep it plain and simple and invent nothing: no extra holes, seams, layers, buttons, labels or parts.

[PET_RULES]
Real fur with visible strands, natural anatomy, natural eyes. The expression comes from the action. No cartoon eyes, no human-like smile.

[TEXT_RULES]
Do not render any text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, icons or UI in the image. Keep the stated area clear and low in detail for typography that will be added later.

[REFERENCE_RULES]
Use each attached reference only for the purpose stated next to it. Never copy the product, branding or text from a style, expression or layout reference.

[AVOID]
wrong product geometry, extra or missing parts, invented buttons or screens, glowing product, glowing water window, text, watermark, logo, badge, certification mark, Wi-Fi icon, duplicate product, floating objects, deformed hands, extra fingers, cartoon pet, waxy fur, flat lighting, orange colour cast, HDR halo, collage, border
